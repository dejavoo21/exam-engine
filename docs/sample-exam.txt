# Sample Exam - Biology Basics

1. What is the basic unit of life?
a) Atom
b) Cell
c) Molecule
d) Organism
Answer: b
Explanation: The cell is the smallest unit of life that can function independently. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells.

2. Which organelle is responsible for producing energy in the cell?
a) Nucleus
b) Mitochondria
c) Ribosome
d) Golgi apparatus
Answer: b
Explanation: Mitochondria is known as the powerhouse of the cell because it produces ATP (adenosine triphosphate), which provides energy for cellular activities.

3. What is the process by which plants convert sunlight into chemical energy?
a) Respiration
b) Photosynthesis
c) Fermentation
d) Decomposition
Answer: b
Explanation: Photosynthesis is the process where plants use light energy, water, and carbon dioxide to produce glucose and oxygen. It occurs mainly in the leaves of plants.

4. Which of the following is a prokaryote?
a) Human
b) Bacteria
c) Plant
d) Fungi
Answer: b
Explanation: Bacteria are prokaryotes because they lack a membrane-bound nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles. They are single-celled organisms with DNA floating freely in the cytoplasm.

5. What is the function of the ribosome?
a) Photosynthesis
b) Storage
c) Protein synthesis
d) Energy production
Answer: c
Explanation: Ribosomes are the cellular structures responsible for protein synthesis. They read messenger RNA (mRNA) and translate it into proteins by assembling amino acids in the correct order.

6. Which blood type is the universal donor?
a) AB+
b) AB-
c) O+
d) O-
Answer: d
Explanation: O- (O negative) blood type is the universal donor because it lacks A and B antigens and the Rh factor, so it can be given to anyone without causing an immune reaction.

7. What is the main function of hemoglobin?
a) Fight infections
b) Transport oxygen
c) Regulate body temperature
d) Digest food
Answer: b
Explanation: Hemoglobin is a protein in red blood cells that binds to oxygen in the lungs and carries it throughout the body to supply oxygen to tissues and organs.

8. Which hormone is responsible for regulating blood sugar levels?
a) Insulin
b) Thyroxine
c) Cortisol
d) Adrenaline
Answer: a
Explanation: Insulin is produced by the pancreas and lowers blood sugar levels by allowing cells to take up glucose from the bloodstream and use it for energy or storage.

9. What is the process by which water moves into cells?
a) Active transport
b) Diffusion
c) Osmosis
d) Exocytosis
Answer: c
Explanation: Osmosis is the movement of water across a semipermeable membrane from an area of higher water concentration to an area of lower water concentration (more solute).

10. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of living organisms?
a) Growth
b) Reproduction
c) Inability to adapt
d) Response to environment
Answer: c
Explanation: Inability to adapt is NOT a characteristic of living organisms. Living things have the ability to adapt to their environment through evolution and individual behavioral changes. All living organisms can grow, reproduce, and respond to their environment.
